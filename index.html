Policy

Cancellation, Refund& Rescheduling Policy

We understand that travel plans can change. We aim to provide complete flexibility and peace of mind when booking your private experience.

Cancellations

  • Guests may cancel their booking at any time before the experience begins.
  • All cancellations are eligible for a 100% refund, regardless of notice.
  • No cancellation penalties or deductions will be applied.

Multiple-outing packages

  • If a guest cancels before their first outing, the entire package is fully refundable.
  • If a package has already begun, any remaining, uncompleted outings will be refunded. Completed outings are not refundable.

Rescheduling

  • Guests may request a date change at no additional charge.
  • Rescheduling is subject to availability and mutual agreement.

Refund processing

  • Approved refunds will be initiated within seven business days.
  • Refunds will be returned through the original payment method where possible.
