Destinations Destination 01 — Abu Dhabi Corniche

The Founder’s Memorial

A quiet landmark on the Corniche, created to honour Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan, the founding father of the UAE.

Book this destination
01 — Overview

Aboutthis place

Set among gardens of native plants, the memorial tells the story of Sheikh Zayed through art, landscape and reflection. Hasher helps you read what you see, from the symbolism of the artwork to the values it represents.

The memorial opened to the public in April 2018, during the Year of Zayed that marked the centenary of his birth.

02 — What you will discover

Highlights

01

The Constellation

The centrepiece, created by artist Ralph Helmick: 1,327 geometric shapes hung from 1,110 cables that come together as a three-dimensional portrait as you move around it.

02

Gardens of native plants

The grounds are planted with trees and plants from the UAE, reflecting Sheikh Zayed’s love of nature and the land.

03

Visitor centre

An exhibition space that introduces his life, values and legacy before or after you see the artwork.

04

Walkway and skyline

A walkway around the pavilion offers views of the artwork and of the Abu Dhabi skyline.
